# Metrics

The North Star metric is **qualified savings discovered per week**. This is better than DAU because an AI spend audit is not a daily-use product. The tool wins when it repeatedly finds real, financially meaningful savings opportunities for startup teams.

Three input metrics drive the North Star. First, audit completion rate: users who start the form and reach results. Second, high-savings audit rate: completed audits with more than $500/month savings. Third, consultation booking rate from high-savings users. These map directly to product usefulness and Credex revenue potential.

I would instrument form starts, form completions, total monthly savings, email captures, public share visits, and consultation clicks first. I would also track which tools most often create savings so Credex can shape inventory and content.

A pivot decision triggers if 300 completed audits produce fewer than 10 high-savings leads or fewer than 3 consultation requests. That would mean the product is either reaching the wrong audience, the audit logic is not finding strong opportunities, or users do not trust the recommendation enough to take action.
