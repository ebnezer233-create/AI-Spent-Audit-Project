# GTM

The exact target user is a founder, CTO, or engineering manager at a seed to Series A startup with 5–80 employees that has started paying for multiple AI tools but has no finance owner dedicated to SaaS optimization. They likely use Cursor, Copilot, ChatGPT Team, Claude, and direct API usage. They feel the bill is growing but do not yet know which spend is productive.

Right before wanting this tool, they are probably searching or reading posts about “Cursor pricing,” “Claude Team vs Pro,” “ChatGPT Team worth it,” “OpenAI API cost optimization,” or “how much should startups spend on AI tools.” Online, they hang out in Indie Hackers, r/SaaS, r/startups, Hacker News, YC founder circles, Buildspace-style Discords, AI engineering Slack groups, and X lists around AI builders.

To get the first 100 users with $0 paid budget, I would start with direct founder outreach using a useful angle: “I built a free audit that tells you whether your AI stack is oversized. Want me to run yours?” I would post teardown threads with anonymized examples: “A 7-person startup was wasting $420/mo on AI subscriptions.” I would reply to pricing complaint posts on X and Reddit with a useful calculation, not spam. I would also ask three early users for permission to publish their anonymized audit as a mini case study.

The unfair Credex distribution channel is access to real discounted AI credit inventory. Most optimization tools can only say “downgrade.” Credex can say “you are using the right tool, but you are paying retail — here is a cheaper path.”

Week-1 traction if this works: 100 audits completed, 30 emails captured, 8 high-savings leads over $500/month, 4 consultation bookings, and at least 2 users sharing their public audit URL.
